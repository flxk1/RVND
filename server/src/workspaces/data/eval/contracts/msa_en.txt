MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is made between Quellwerk Software GmbH (the
"Supplier") and Hanse Logistik SE (the "Customer"). Individual projects are
ordered under a Statement of Work.

This Agreement comes into force on 2026-08-01.

1. Definitions. "Deliverables" means the work products specified in a
Statement of Work. "Acceptance" means the Customer's written confirmation
that Deliverables conform to the agreed specification.

2. Delivery. The Supplier shall deliver the Deliverables in accordance with
the schedule in the applicable Statement of Work.

3. Acceptance testing. The Customer shall complete acceptance testing within
21 days of the delivery.

4. Remediation. The Supplier shall remedy any reported non-conformity within
30 days of the notification.

5. Payment. The Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of the
invoice date.

6. Late payment. Where the outstanding amount exceeds EUR 50,000, the
Supplier may suspend further performance.

7. Subcontracting. The Supplier must not subcontract material obligations
without the prior written consent of the Customer.

8. Liability. Neither party's aggregate liability shall exceed the fees paid
in the twelve months preceding the claim.

9. Governing law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Federal
Republic of Germany.
