MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Confidentiality Agreement is made between Aurora Robotics Ltd (the
"Discloser") and Brandt Ventures GmbH (the "Recipient").

This Agreement is effective as of 2026-09-15.

1. Definitions. "Confidential Information" means any non-public information
disclosed in connection with the Purpose. "Purpose" means the evaluation of a
potential joint venture in warehouse robotics.

2. Use. The Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely for the
Purpose.

3. Protection. The Recipient shall protect Confidential Information with at
least the same degree of care it applies to its own confidential material.

4. Disclosure. The Recipient must not disclose Confidential Information to
any third party without the prior written consent of the Discloser.

5. Return. The Recipient shall return or destroy all Confidential
Information within 14 days of a written request.

6. Permitted disclosure. The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information
where required by law or court order, provided it gives prompt notice.

7. Term. The non-disclosure obligations survive for five years after the
effective date.

8. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
