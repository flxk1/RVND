DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement is made between Norddata Services GmbH (the
"Processor") and Beispielkunde AG (the "Controller"), together governed by
Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

This Agreement is effective as of 2026-07-01.

1. Definitions. "Services" means the hosting and analytics services described
in Annex 1. "Sub-processor" means any third party engaged by the Processor to
process personal data.

2. Instructions. The Processor shall process personal data only on documented
instructions from the Controller.

3. Confidentiality. The Processor shall ensure that persons authorised to
process the personal data have committed themselves to confidentiality.

4. Breach notification. The Processor shall notify the Controller of a
personal data breach no later than 72 hours after the personal data breach.

5. Sub-processing. The Processor must not engage a Sub-processor without the
prior written authorisation of the Controller.

6. Deletion. The Processor shall delete all personal data within 30 days of
the termination, unless Union or Member State law requires storage.

7. Audits. The Controller may conduct audits of the processing activities
once per calendar year.

8. Governing law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Federal
Republic of Germany.
