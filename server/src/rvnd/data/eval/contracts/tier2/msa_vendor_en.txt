MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is entered into between Nordwind Digital GmbH
("Vendor") and Carlsen & Brandt KG ("Client") for the provision of managed
IT services under individual Statements of Work.

This Agreement shall become effective on 1 October 2026.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Services" means the managed hosting, monitoring and support services
described in the applicable Statement of Work.

1.2 "Service Levels" means the availability and response targets set out in
Schedule 2.

2. PERFORMANCE

2.1 The Vendor shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care and
in accordance with the Service Levels.

2.2 The Client shall provide the Vendor with the access credentials required
for the Services. Any failure to provide such credentials suspends the
affected delivery dates for the duration of the failure.

3. INVOICING

3.1 The Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within ten (10) business
days of receipt of the invoice.

3.2 Where an invoice remains unpaid for more than 30 days after the due
date, the Vendor may suspend the Services.

4. SUBCONTRACTING

4.1 The Vendor shall not engage subcontractors for material parts of the
Services except as set out in Clause 7.

5. TERM

5.1 The initial term of this Agreement is twenty-four (24) months.

6. GOVERNING LAW

6.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it
are governed by German law.
