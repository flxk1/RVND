# Rvnd contributor licence agreement

By submitting a contribution to Rvnd, you agree that:

1. You have the legal right to submit the contribution.
2. You assign to the project owner all copyright you hold in the contribution.
3. Where an assignment is ineffective, you grant the project owner an
   exclusive, irrevocable, worldwide, perpetual, transferable,
   sublicensable, royalty-free licence to use, modify, distribute, relicense,
   and otherwise exploit the contribution in any form.
4. You retain the right to use your contribution yourself.
5. You understand that the project may distribute the contribution under
   AGPL-3.0-only and under separate commercial terms.

The assignment, with the exclusive fallback grant, is required so the same
maintained codebase can be offered under both licensing tracks. A contribution
is accepted only after the project owner records the contributor's agreement.

## Recorded assent

For this agreement, the project owner is the account or organization that owns
the canonical repository when the assent is recorded. A pull request records
assent only when its author includes the exact declaration from the repository
pull-request template in the pull-request description. A maintainer verifies
that declaration and applies the `cla:accepted` label before merge. The pull
request and label are the durable assent record.

Contributions made outside that pull-request process are not accepted until the
same declaration is recorded in a repository record controlled by the project
owner. Silence, a commit, or DCO sign-off alone is not assent to this agreement.
